Privacy Policy

Daily Games Hub · Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version: Daily Games Hub has no account and we do not collect any personal information ourselves. Everything the app remembers (favorites, streaks, stats) stays on your phone. The app shows banner and full-screen ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show them, as explained in the “Advertising” section below.

Who we are

Daily Games Hub (“the app”) is published by Daily hub games. You can contact us at worstacc0untkr@gmail.com.

What the app saves on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information in its own storage on your phone:

  • the games you have marked as favorites;
  • the games you have chosen to block;
  • a copy of the latest game list the app downloaded;
  • which games you have ticked off today;
  • your daily streak for each game;
  • how many games you have finished each day, your overall daily streak, and the milestones you have reached (used for the Today and stats screens);
  • the category you were last looking at, whether you have seen the welcome screen and quick tour, and whether you have already been shown the one-time reminder to rate the app.

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone’s settings.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and we do not receive any of the information the ad service below collects. The app has no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no crash-reporting tools that send data to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a small banner ad at the bottom of its own screens and, occasionally (about once for every 10 games you open), a full-screen ad, which may be a short video that you can close. Ads are never shown inside the games. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID (a resettable number that Android gives each phone);
  • your IP address and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • device and app information (phone model, Android version, language, app name and version);
  • how ads perform (whether an ad was shown or tapped), and diagnostic information about the ad service.

This information goes to Google, not to us. We do not see it and cannot use it to identify you. How Google handles it is described in Google’s advertising policy and privacy policy. Google and its partners may use the advertising ID to show ads that are personalised to your interests. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app first shows a consent message from Google and asks for your choice before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings, about & feedback > “Ad privacy choices”, when that button is shown.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone’s settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). Ads will still appear, but they will not be personalised.

The app asks for ads suitable for a general audience (up to “Teen”). It is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly serve personalised ads to them.

Reporting a problem or sending feedback

If you press and hold a game to report a problem, or use the feedback buttons on the About screen, the app opens your own email app with a pre-written message. It contains the game’s name and link, the app version and your phone model, and you can read and change it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever the message contains, and we use it only to look into the problem and reply to you.

Backups

The app can create a backup code containing your favorites, blocked games, streaks, history and settings so you can move them to another phone. The code is created on your phone and shown only to you. It is not uploaded anywhere and we never receive it. It leaves your phone only if you copy it, share it or paste it somewhere yourself. Anyone who has the code could restore your lists on another phone, so keep it somewhere private.

Games and websites you open

The app is a directory that links to daily puzzle games run by other people and companies. When you tap a game, it opens in a browser view inside the app. Those games and websites are not ours. They may collect information such as your IP address, use cookies or analytics, and show advertising, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive that information and do not control how those sites use it. Please read the privacy policy of any game you play.

Internet access and permissions

The app’s own screens work without an internet connection, and a copy of the game list is built into the app. To keep that list up to date, the app may download a small text file containing the names, links and descriptions of the games from [the web address your game list is hosted on, for example yourname.github.io]. This happens in the background when you open the app. No account or personal details are sent with that request. As with any visit to a website, the company that hosts the file can see your IP address and the type of device making the request. We do not receive or store that information, and that host has its own privacy policy.

The app uses the standard internet permission for this, for opening game links and for loading ads. It also uses Android’s advertising ID permission, which lets the ad service read your advertising ID. The app does not ask for any other special permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or notifications.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. Because the games it links to are run by third parties, parents and guardians should check that those games are suitable.

Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information, there is none for us to secure or keep on our servers. Everything the app saves stays on your phone. Information collected by Google for ads is kept under Google’s own policies. If you email us, we keep your message only for as long as we need it to look into the problem and reply, and then delete it.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can erase everything the app has saved by clearing its data or uninstalling it. For information held by Google for advertising, you can use the ad controls described above and in your Google Account at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works, for example by adding features that need more information, we will update this policy before the change is released and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email worstacc0untkr@gmail.com.
