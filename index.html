Privacy Policy

Daily Games Hub · Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version: Daily Games Hub does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

Who we are

Daily Games Hub (“the app”) is published by [your name or business name]. You can contact us at [your email address].

What the app saves on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information in its own storage on your phone:

  • the games you have marked as favorites;
  • the games you have chosen to block;
  • a copy of the latest game list the app downloaded;
  • which games you have ticked off today;
  • your daily streak for each game;
  • the category you were last looking at, and whether you have seen the welcome screen.

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone’s settings.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting tools that send data to us or to anyone else.

Reporting a problem or sending feedback

If you press and hold a game to report a problem, or use the feedback buttons on the About screen, the app opens your own email app with a pre-written message. It contains the game’s name and link, the app version and your phone model, and you can read and change it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever the message contains, and we use it only to look into the problem and reply to you.

Games and websites you open

The app is a directory that links to daily puzzle games run by other people and companies. When you tap a game, it opens in a browser view inside the app. Those games and websites are not ours. They may collect information such as your IP address, use cookies or analytics, and show advertising, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive that information and do not control how those sites use it. Please read the privacy policy of any game you play.

Internet access and permissions

The app’s own screens work without an internet connection, and a copy of the game list is built into the app. To keep that list up to date, the app may download a small text file containing the names, links and descriptions of the games from [the web address your game list is hosted on, for example yourname.github.io]. This happens in the background when you open the app. No account or personal details are sent with that request. As with any visit to a website, the company that hosts the file can see your IP address and the type of device making the request. We do not receive or store that information, and that host has its own privacy policy.

The app uses the standard internet permission for this and for opening game links. It does not ask for any other special permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or notifications.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the games it links to are run by third parties, parents and guardians should check that those games are suitable.

Data security and retention

Because the app does not collect personal information, there is none for us to secure or keep on our servers. Everything the app saves stays on your phone. If you email us, we keep your message only for as long as we need it to look into the problem and reply, and then delete it.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can erase everything the app has saved by clearing its data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works, for example by adding features that need more information, we will update this policy before the change is released and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email [your email address].
